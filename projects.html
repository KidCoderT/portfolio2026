BeanCounter

A Telegram bot that logs an expense in the time it takes to type "purse 5.2". Regex handles about 95% of messages with no API call; ambiguous ones like "5 in purse and 20 from credit" go to Amazon Nova Micro on Bedrock. Wallets answer to any nickname you give them, success is a single 👍 reaction, and the React dashboard signs you in by verifying Telegram's initData signature, so there is no login.

Diabetes Risk Predictor

Built for the Global Innovation Field Trip: a classifier trained on 70,692 CDC survey responses (BRFSS 2015 health indicators) that screens separately for prediabetes and type 2 diabetes, after comparing logistic regression, random forest, XGBoost and LightGBM in the notebook. FastAPI serves the model, a Next.js questionnaire collects the 21 indicators, and every result carries a plain "see a doctor" caveat.

Biryani Battery Reminder

A Windows utility that nags at the right moments: unplug near full, plug in when low, at thresholds you set. It switches to Power Saver on its own at low charge and restores your plan once charging, shows battery health against the original design capacity, follows the system light or dark theme, and lives in the tray. Packaged with Inno Setup, fully offline, AGPL.

Vision-controlled robot car

A four-day build at IISc that ended in a two-wheel robot steered by a camera. MediaPipe hand gestures and YOLO detections on a laptop become single-word UDP commands to an ESP8266, with per-wheel PWM calibration through a web dashboard because real wheels never match. Along the way: a Node.js MQTT broker with auth, captive-portal WiFi setup, and a YOLO v8 versus v11 size-and-speed benchmark.
